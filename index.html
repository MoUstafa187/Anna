For Mami, with all my love

Mi amor,

Somewhere along the way, I realized I could not imagine a day without talking to you or seeing your face. You had become part of the shape of my days, and I wanted to give what we have my full attention. I chose to close the door on pursuing other women because I want to focus on you and on the relationship we are building.

With you, a playful, childlike part of me comes out. We make faces on video calls, send drawings, laugh at our own jokes, and turn small moments into something that belongs only to us. I love that version of me, and I love that I get to share him with you.

I love your kindness and the care you put into your son. I see how hard you keep trying for him, even when things feel difficult. I admire your intelligence, your elegance, your love of makeup, and the beautiful woman I see when I look at you. I love your whole self—not only how you look, and not only what you do for me. You do not have to earn my love by being perfect or by carrying everything alone.

When you feel sad or overwhelmed, I wish I could hold you. When you worry you are the bad person, I want to listen before I ask you to defend yourself. I know the distance can make it harder to feel reassured; a screen cannot offer the same comfort as being there. I want to meet that distance with honesty, respect, and consistency—not with a demand that you simply stop feeling afraid.

I know I will still have things to learn. I want to be a partner who listens, takes responsibility when I am wrong, speaks to you with care, and keeps working through difficult moments with you. I want my choices, over time, to show you that I choose us. I do not want to be a passing chapter in your life. I want to build a future with you through real, everyday actions.

I hope we can build a future with you and your son—a loving home where he has room to grow and where we can take care of one another. I still hold our family dream gently: three boys in total, including your son, two more boys we hope for together, and one little girl. I dream of that with you, while knowing the future is something we hope for and work toward, together.

Te extraño, Mi amor. Quiero cuidarte, escucharte, hacerte reír. Quiero besarte. Te necesito. Thank you for letting me know the real you, and for making room for the real me.

All my love,
Your Mi amor
